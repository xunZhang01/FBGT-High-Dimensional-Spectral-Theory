Overview
This repository contains FBGT High-Dimensional Spectral Theory (《FBGT 高维谱理论》), a four-volume mathematical framework developed over approximately one year of sustained theoretical work. The framework aims to establish a unified spectral foundation for mathematics, replacing set-theoretic foundations with a spectral-theoretic one.

The central claim is that the spectrum of a formal system is determined by geometry and boundary conditions, not by arithmetic encoding. This is expressed in the main theorem:

\sigma(T_\Sigma) = \Lambda_d(\Sigma) \cup \left[\frac{(n-1)^2}{4}, \infty\right),

where Λd(Σ) corresponds to provable propositions, and the continuous part corresponds to independent propositions.

Structure
The work is organized into four volumes and eighteen chapters:

Volume I — Ontology: Super-algebraic sets A=(F,T,∼), cognitive manifolds M=H4/Γ, the Tarski boundary layer BB, and the cognitive supercategory CogTopCogTop.

Volume II — Spectral Theory: Self-adjoint operators, spectral decomposition, McKean's theorem, Mazzeo–Melrose cusp expansion, the logical characteristic operator TΣ=−ΔX+VΣ, and the spectral decomposition theorem.

Volume III — Applications: Spectral analyses of the Four Color Theorem, P≠NP, the Riemann Hypothesis, and the Hodge Conjecture.

Volume IV — Metatheory: The category SpecSpec of spectral spaces, completeness and consistency, Gödel's second incompleteness theorem, and the philosophical program.

Main Results
Four classical problems unified: Four Color (col=dind=4), P≠NP (spectral separation), Riemann Hypothesis (spectral realness), Hodge Conjecture (spectral identity).

Common invariant: All four problems share the invariant H5(Σ)>0.

Gödel incompleteness geometrized: Incompleteness corresponds to spectral gaps on the Tarski boundary layer.

Hilbert–Pólya conjecture realized: The self-adjoint operator H=TΣRH gives a concrete spectral realization of the Riemann zeros.

Boundary Conditions and Open Problems
The framework explicitly declares its boundaries:

Mathematical gap: The explicit functional structure of H5(Σ) has not yet been constructed.

Relative consistency: Consistency holds relative to ZFC; self-consistency cannot be proven internally.

Halting theorem failure: On super-algebraic sets with infinite subfamily collections, the halting theorem's premises fail. This is the source of the framework's incompleteness.

These are declared as structural boundaries, not defects.
